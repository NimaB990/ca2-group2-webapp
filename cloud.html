02 / Subject

Computing that moves with you.

Cloud computing delivers technology resources over the internet, making it easier to build, scale, and collaborate from almost anywhere.

Concept 01

On demand

Access servers, storage, and software when you need them without maintaining all the hardware yourself.

Concept 02

Scalable

Resources can grow with demand, helping applications stay useful as more people use them.

Concept 03

Connected

Teams can share tools and data across locations, supporting collaboration and faster delivery.

Concept 04

Efficient

Shared infrastructure can reduce setup time and help organizations use resources more efficiently.
